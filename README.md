# Spendlog

A closer look at your money.

Spendlog is a personal project to record, organize and explore everyday financial activity.

It aims to make personal spending more visible, helping you understand your financial habits, see where your money goes and get a clearer picture of your monthly cash flow.

## Goals

- Record and organize income and expenses.
- Categorize transactions to understand spending patterns.
- Track monthly expenses and compare spending over time.
- Gain visibility into personal cash flow.
- Make more informed financial decisions based on spending habits.

Features, architecture and implementation details will evolve as the project grows.
